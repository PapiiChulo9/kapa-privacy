KAPA — Delete My Data

You can delete your KAPA data in two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open KAPA → Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes all transactions, budgets, and settings from your device immediately.

Option 2 — Email request

To delete your subscription record from our servers, email us at hello@duxiedev.com with subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will delete your record within 30 days.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. KAPA Pro is billed by Google Play, so to stop it, cancel in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. If you ask us to delete your server record while a subscription is still active, the record is created again the next time KAPA checks your subscription, so please cancel first.

What gets deleted

  • In-app deletion: all transactions, budgets, scores, settings, and profile data — permanently and immediately
  • Server deletion: your subscription record held by Duxie Dev (plan, status, dates, Google Play purchase token, and your email address if you provided one)
  • Retained temporarily: crash logs held by Sentry (auto-deleted within 90 days)
